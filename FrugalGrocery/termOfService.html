2. What FrugalGrocery does

Service description

FrugalGrocery helps you build and compare grocery shopping lists based on prices you provide from different stores. You can add product and price information in three ways:

  • Receipt upload or scan: you may upload or scan a receipt, and an AI system extracts product names and prices, which are then associated with a store you select or create.
  • Price tag upload or scan: you may upload or scan images of price tags, and an AI system extracts the product and price, which are then associated with a store you select or create.
  • Manual entry: you can manually type in product names, quantities, and prices for the stores you choose.

The App organizes this information so you can create, compare, and optimize grocery lists based on your own data.
