Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 22, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Salvatore Castellitti ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use the NAMETBD mobile application (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy reflects our "Offline First" and "Privacy First" approach.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT require user accounts. You do not need to provide an email address, phone number, or social media login to use the App.

A. Local Data (Stored on your device)

The following data is stored locally on your device and is NOT transmitted to our servers:

  • Player Names: Names you enter for players during game setup.
  • Game State: Scores, current round progress, and active game settings.
  • App Preferences: Language settings, sound toggles, and theme choices.

B. Purchase Data (Third Party)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases (the "Lifetime" unlock). When you make a purchase:

  • Apple/Google processes the payment. We do not have access to your credit card information.
  • RevenueCat may assign an anonymous App User ID to track entitlement status (i.e., verifying you have purchased the premium version).
  • This data is used solely to unlock the features you paid for.

3. How We Use Your Information

Since we collect minimal data, our use of it is limited:

  • To provide the App's core functionality (gameplay).
  • To verify premium purchase status (via RevenueCat).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Data: All game data and player names are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete this data.

Purchase Data: Transaction records are maintained by Apple/Google and RevenueCat for accounting and entitlement verification purposes in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services that are not owned or controlled by us. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18+. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from a child under age 13 without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information.
