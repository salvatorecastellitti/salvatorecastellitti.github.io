Game Modes

Discover a variety of games designed to entertain any group:

Five Second Rule

Think fast! You have 5 seconds to name 3 things in a category. Can you handle the pressure?

Impostor

Find out who is faking it. One person doesn't know the secret word - catch them if you can!

Never Have I Ever

Reveal secrets and get to know your friends better with classic and spicy prompts.

Most Likely To

Vote on who fits the description best. Expect laughs and friendly debates.

Truth or Dare

The classic party staple. Choose truth or face the consequences!

Psychiatrist

A role-playing deduction game where the 'psychiatrist' must diagnose the group's shared ailment.

Physical challenges and mini-games to get everyone moving and interacting.
