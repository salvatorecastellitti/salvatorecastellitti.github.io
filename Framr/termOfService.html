2. Service description

What Framr does

Framr is a mobile app that analyzes videos you provide and extracts meaningful still frames using AI. There are two processing modes:

  • On-device mode: The video is processed entirely on your device using a local AI model (Qwen 2.5 via llama.rn). No video data is sent to any server.
  • Cloud mode: The video is uploaded to our cloud infrastructure where an AI model analyzes it and returns extracted frames. The video is permanently deleted from our servers as soon as processing is complete. We do not retain, store, or use your video after that point.
