3. FACE DATA COLLECTION, USE, DISCLOSURE, SHARING, AND RETENTION

In Short: MiniMe processes facial imagery from photos you upload solely to provide AI-powered enhancement, transformation, and video generation. Face data is not used for identification, is shared only with our AI service providers for processing, and is retained only as long as necessary to complete the service.

What face data does the app collect?

The app collects face data only when you voluntarily upload photos that contain faces. Specifically:

  • Facial imagery: The photos you upload may contain images of faces (yours or others). We do not separately extract or store face templates, faceprints, or biometric identifiers. The face data we process is the facial imagery contained within the image files you provide.
  • No continuous or passive collection: We do not collect face data from your device camera in the background. Face data is collected only when you choose to upload a photo for AI processing.

Planned uses of face data

We use the face data contained in your photos only for the following purposes:

  • AI-powered image enhancement and transformation: To apply our "glow up" and enhancement effects to your photos using artificial intelligence.
  • AI-generated video creation: When you use the video generation feature (with credits), we use the facial imagery from your photos to generate short videos via third-party AI video generation models.
  • Delivery of requested services: To return the enhanced images or generated videos to you within the app.

We do not use face data for identification, authentication, advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than providing the AI enhancement and video generation features you request.

Sharing and storage of face data

Third-party sharing: Face data (in the form of the photos you upload) is shared with third-party AI service providers that we use to perform the processing. Specifically:

  • Photos containing faces are sent to our image enhancement AI providers to produce enhanced or transformed images.
  • For video generation, photos are sent to Alibaba's video generation models to create videos from your photos.

These providers process your data solely to perform the requested service under our instructions. We do not sell face data or share it with third parties for their own marketing or other purposes.

Where face data is stored: Your photos (and thus any face data) are transmitted to our systems and to our AI service providers' servers for processing. We aim to delete uploaded photos from our servers and request deletion from our providers' systems shortly after processing is complete. Face data is not stored in a separate, long-term face database.

How long face data is retained

Retention period: Face data is retained only as long as necessary to complete the service you requested:

  • Photos uploaded for AI processing are typically deleted from our servers shortly after processing is complete.
  • We do not retain face data for long-term storage. We aim to delete or have deleted any copies held by our AI service providers after generation is complete.
  • Any generated content (enhanced images or videos) that you save may be stored on your device; we do not retain that content on our servers beyond any temporary period needed for you to download it.

When we have no ongoing need to process your face data, we delete it or ensure our providers do so. We do not retain face data for longer than necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this section.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
